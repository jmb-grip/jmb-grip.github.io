The next generation of CRISPR diagnostics is here

GRIP’s diagnostic platform is built on a CRISPR protease engine. The tests are fast, operate at room temperature, require no nucleic acid pre-amplification, and are readily adaptable to multiple existing readout formats — delivering molecular-grade accuracy wherever testing is needed.

The GRIP platform at a glance

Protease-powered precision

Our next-gen CRISPR protease uses stable proteins as reporter substrates, avoiding false positives from nucleases.

Results in minutes

Our enzymes rapidly amplify signal and work at room temperature, giving lab-grade results in just minutes.

Pathogen and sample agnostic

Detect bacteria, viruses, or fungi. Saliva, swabs, and urine samples are easily integrated, enabling a variety of sample inputs.

Plug-and-play manufacturing

GRIP’s protein-based reagents allow for seamless integration into proven and robust immunoassay pipelines.

GRIP’s CRISPR protease enzyme

A flexible platform technology

GRIP's platform brings molecular test precision to multiple readout methods, making the technology adaptable to the differing needs of PON and POC diagnostics. Readout methods include lateral flow assays, electrochemical tests, and even simple colorimetric assays.
